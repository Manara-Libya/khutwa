## How to verify (one call per number)

1. Call the number from a Libyan phone.
2. Record: the date and time, whether someone answered, the service name they gave, the hours they work, which cities they cover, and whether they're free of charge.
3. Ask only what the service offers. **Don't describe a real person's situation.** If they ask, say we're students preparing a list of services for a student project.
4. If nobody answers, try once more at another time. If they still don't answer, list it under "Tried, not verified".
